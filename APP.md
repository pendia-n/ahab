# Ahab

Ahab helps creators, founders and businesses prepare and generate a vertical video from approved words and references. Customers choose one of six video formats, buy non-expiring allowances and create each video when ready. Clear prices, input review and technical-failure restoration reduce uncertainty before a paid generation.

The application is in `ahab/`. See `ahab/APP.md` for product rules, model routing, pricing, privacy, API behavior, tests and deployment. The product provides a guided creation workflow rather than a customer-facing model catalog.
