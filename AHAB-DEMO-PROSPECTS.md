## Production and raw cost

Build 15-second vertical (9:16) demos at 720p. In every row, A-roll is a single generated presenter clip: **HeyGen Avatar IV** (`heygen/avatar-iv`) at **$0.05 per billed output second**, based on [OpenRouter’s model listing](https://openrouter.ai/heygen/avatar-iv-20260625). The finished duration is 15s; only the generated seconds cost OpenRouter credits. A-roll lengths are cost targets: check the selected duration and final billed output before assuming an exact charge. Write the script yourself and reuse your own or an authorized presenter portrait. No separate text, image, or music model is budgeted.

B-roll is real phone footage from the shop or footage you have permission to film and use. It costs **$0 in OpenRouter credits**. If the presenter voice continues over B-roll, cover the image while keeping the audio in your editor. Put the real product on screen early. Label the presenter as AI and treat the scene as a scripted demo, not a customer review.
